# WutPack launch kit

Use the public page as the primary link:

## One-line pitch

Turn a new Mac into a persistent AI office for documents, spreadsheets,
presentations, PDFs, research, data, diagrams, and twelve specialist workflows.

## Show HN

**Title**

> Show HN: WutPack – turn a fresh Mac into a persistent AI office

**Post**

> I wanted a new Mac to be useful for serious knowledge work immediately, not
> after another afternoon of finding PDF tools, Office libraries, browsers,
> diagram renderers, data packages, and agent integrations.
>
> WutPack is a free, MIT-licensed installer and workflow layer for Codex and
> Claude Code. It installs both CLIs by default, keeps its environments in stable
> locations, and adds twelve specialists for research, spreadsheets, technical
> decks, PDFs, data, diagrams, documentation, building, debugging, review, and
> release work.
>
> The installer and all manifests are readable. It does not sign into accounts
> or copy credentials. The current release is tested from clean Apple-silicon
> and Intel macOS runners and builds real PPTX, XLSX, and SVG artifacts before CI
> passes.
>
> I would value feedback on the package scope, the beginner setup experience,
> and which non-coding knowledge-work scenario should be added next.
