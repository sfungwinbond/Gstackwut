# Source quality ladder

Use the highest available rung and explain when a decision rests on a weaker one.

1. Governing standard, law, regulator, audited filing, or original dataset.
2. Official vendor documentation, release notes, or first-party announcement.
3. Peer-reviewed paper or reproducible technical report.
4. Reputable reporting with named sources and linked evidence.
5. Expert analysis with disclosed methods and incentives.
6. Community discussion, anecdote, or unattributed summary.

For conflicts, compare scope, measurement date, definitions, sample, and incentives before deciding which source controls.
