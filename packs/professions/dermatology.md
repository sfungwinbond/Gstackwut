# Dermatology operations and evidence toolpack

Use this pack for clinic access analysis, aggregate outcomes, literature updates,
image-asset governance, education drafts, and practice-performance reporting.

## Workflow

1. State the non-diagnostic question, intended audience, and reviewer.
2. Remove identifiers and strip unnecessary image metadata before processing.
3. Profile access, throughput, follow-up, or aggregate outcome data.
4. Compare current primary evidence and clearly state population limits.
5. Produce editable education, operations, and measurement artifacts.

## Deliverables

- Clinic-capacity or quality model
- Evidence brief with dated sources
- Governed asset register and review-ready education draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not diagnose from an image, classify a lesion, or recommend
treatment. Images and health data must be de-identified and access-controlled.

## Starter prompt

Use the dermatology operations and evidence toolpack. Work only with fictional
or properly de-identified material and remove unnecessary image metadata. Focus
on evidence synthesis, clinic operations, aggregate quality, or education. Do
not diagnose from an image, classify a lesion, or recommend treatment. Cite
current primary sources, label uncertainty, preserve editable outputs, and mark
all clinical language for review by a licensed dermatologist.
