# Prosthodontics practice and lab toolpack

Use this pack for lab turnaround analysis, inventory and capacity models,
evidence summaries, workflow mapping, and patient-education drafts.

## Workflow

1. Define the practice or laboratory question and responsible reviewer.
2. De-identify case data and separate financial assumptions from observations.
3. Model turnaround, remakes, utilization, inventory, or scenario economics.
4. Map prescription-to-delivery handoffs and exception paths.
5. Produce editable operational and educational materials for approval.

## Deliverables

- Formula-driven practice or laboratory workbook
- Editable case-flow diagram
- Evidence or education draft with sources and assumptions

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not create a patient-specific treatment plan, prescribe a
device, or interpret imaging. A licensed dental professional must approve use.

## Starter prompt

Use the prosthodontics practice and lab toolpack. Work only with fictional or
properly de-identified material. Separate measured operations data, financial
assumptions, and professional judgment. Do not create a patient-specific plan,
prescribe a device, or interpret imaging. Build editable models and documents,
cite current primary evidence, and add explicit approval gates for the licensed
prosthodontist and laboratory owner.
