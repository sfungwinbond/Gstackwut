# Anesthesiology quality and flow toolpack

Use this pack for aggregate operating-room flow, staffing scenarios, protocol
evidence, quality measurement, and policy-document maintenance.

## Workflow

1. Define the operational question, cohort, metric, and clinical owner.
2. Confirm de-identification and validate timestamps, exclusions, and units.
3. Analyze aggregate delays, utilization, staffing, or quality indicators.
4. Compare current protocol evidence and document jurisdictional limits.
5. Produce a review-ready model, process map, and controlled policy draft.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible flow or quality analysis
- Scenario workbook with explicit assumptions
- Versioned protocol evidence table and draft document

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not calculate patient dosing, choose an anesthetic plan, or
monitor a patient. Clinical protocols require local governance and signoff.

## Starter prompt

Use the anesthesiology quality and flow toolpack. Analyze only fictional or
properly de-identified aggregate information. Validate timestamps, units,
exclusions, denominators, and uncertainty. Do not calculate patient dosing,
select an anesthetic plan, or provide monitoring instructions. Cite current
primary sources and local policy constraints, and deliver editable artifacts
with approval gates for anesthesia and quality leadership.
