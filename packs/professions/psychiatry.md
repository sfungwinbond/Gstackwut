# Psychiatry evidence and operations toolpack

Use this pack for de-identified outcomes analysis, literature surveillance,
service-capacity planning, policy comparison, and patient-education drafts.

## Workflow

1. Define the operational or research question and the licensed reviewer.
2. Remove direct identifiers before any analysis or model-facing work.
3. Build a primary-source evidence table with dates and population limits.
4. Analyze aggregate outcomes with uncertainty and missingness made visible.
5. Produce a review draft that separates evidence, inference, and open questions.

## Deliverables

- Evidence matrix with source dates and applicability notes
- Reproducible aggregate analysis and data-quality report
- Accessible education or policy draft awaiting clinical approval

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not diagnose, assess an individual, recommend medication,
or replace a licensed clinician. Do not provide identifiable health information.

## Starter prompt

Use the psychiatry evidence and operations toolpack. Work only with de-identified
or fictional material. Separate reported evidence from inference, cite current
primary sources, quantify uncertainty, and create reproducible analysis where
data is supplied. Do not diagnose an individual, recommend medication, or make
an unsupervised clinical decision. Mark every clinical statement as a draft for
review by a licensed psychiatrist and identify the evidence that could change it.
