# Surgery quality and operations toolpack

Use this pack for aggregate perioperative quality analysis, operating-room
capacity, evidence summaries, process maps, and implementation communication.

## Workflow

1. Define the metric, cohort, time window, exclusions, and accountable reviewer.
2. Validate de-identification, observation grain, missingness, and denominators.
3. Analyze delays, cancellations, utilization, or aggregate outcomes.
4. Map the perioperative workflow and its control points.
5. Package findings as an editable model, diagram, and decision brief.

## Deliverables

- Auditable quality or capacity workbook
- Editable perioperative process map
- Evidence-backed improvement brief with balancing measures

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not select a procedure, interpret a patient case, or direct
care. Clinical decisions and quality conclusions require authorized review.

## Starter prompt

Use the surgery quality and operations toolpack. Analyze only de-identified or
fictional information. Verify denominators, cohort definitions, missingness, and
uncertainty before drawing conclusions. Use primary clinical or regulatory
sources for current claims. Do not select procedures or advise on an individual
case. Deliver editable evidence, analysis, and workflow artifacts with explicit
review gates for the responsible surgical and quality leaders.
