# Radiology operations and quality toolpack

Use this pack for de-identified turnaround analysis, report-template governance,
workflow mapping, quality metrics, and evidence or policy drafts.

## Workflow

1. Define modality, service boundary, timestamp semantics, and reviewers.
2. Remove identifiers and avoid copying diagnostic images into unapproved tools.
3. Analyze aggregate turnaround, queues, addenda, or communication reliability.
4. Map acquisition-to-report handoffs and exception paths.
5. Produce controlled templates, evidence notes, and improvement artifacts.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible turnaround or quality analysis
- Editable imaging-service workflow diagram
- Versioned report-template or policy draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not interpret an image, generate a diagnostic report, or
recommend follow-up for an individual. Radiologist review is mandatory.

## Starter prompt

Use the radiology operations and quality toolpack. Work only with fictional or
properly de-identified aggregate information, and do not copy diagnostic images
into unapproved systems. Validate modality, timestamp semantics, denominators,
and missingness. Do not interpret images, generate diagnostic reports, or advise
individual follow-up. Deliver auditable analysis, diagrams, and controlled drafts
for review by licensed radiology and quality leaders.
