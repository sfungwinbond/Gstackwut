# Emergency medicine operations toolpack

Use this pack for de-identified arrival and flow analysis, staffing scenarios,
protocol evidence, incident-learning synthesis, and improvement communication.

## Workflow

1. Define the service question, time horizon, denominator, and accountable owner.
2. Validate timestamps, duplicate encounters, disposition logic, and missingness.
3. Analyze aggregate demand, waits, boarding, capacity, or process reliability.
4. Map handoffs, queues, escalation paths, and balancing measures.
5. Deliver auditable analysis and a review-ready improvement package.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible flow and capacity analysis
- Editable queue and handoff map
- Evidence-backed improvement brief and measurement plan

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not triage a person, diagnose an emergency, or recommend
treatment. Anyone needing urgent help must use local emergency services.

## Starter prompt

Use the emergency medicine operations toolpack. Work only with fictional or
properly de-identified aggregate data. Validate timestamps, encounter grain,
denominators, missingness, and uncertainty. Focus on operations, quality, and
evidence—not live triage, diagnosis, or treatment. For any urgent real-world
situation, direct the user to local emergency services. Produce editable,
auditable artifacts for emergency medicine and operations leadership review.
