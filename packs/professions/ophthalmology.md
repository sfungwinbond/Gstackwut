# Ophthalmology clinic and evidence toolpack

Use this pack for de-identified clinic-capacity analysis, aggregate outcomes,
evidence summaries, pathway mapping, and patient-education drafts.

## Workflow

1. Define the clinic or research question and licensed reviewer.
2. De-identify records and strip unnecessary metadata from permitted assets.
3. Validate visits, procedures, follow-up windows, measures, and missingness.
4. Compare primary evidence and document population or device limitations.
5. Produce editable models, pathways, and plain-language drafts.

## Deliverables

- Clinic access or aggregate outcomes workbook
- Editable pathway and handoff map
- Evidence-backed education or policy draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not interpret an eye image, diagnose disease, recommend a
procedure, or prescribe treatment. Licensed ophthalmologist review is required.

## Starter prompt

Use the ophthalmology clinic and evidence toolpack. Work only with fictional or
properly de-identified aggregate information. Validate measures, units,
follow-up windows, denominators, missingness, and uncertainty. Do not interpret
eye images, diagnose disease, recommend procedures, or prescribe treatment.
Cite current primary evidence and deliver editable operational or educational
artifacts for review by a licensed ophthalmologist.
