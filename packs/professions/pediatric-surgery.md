# Pediatric surgery quality toolpack

Use this pack for de-identified service-quality work, capacity planning, pathway
mapping, literature summaries, and family-facing communication drafts.

## Workflow

1. Define age range, service boundary, metric, and licensed reviewers.
2. Apply heightened privacy controls for minors and use aggregate data.
3. Validate denominators, transfers, follow-up windows, and missingness.
4. Map family and clinical handoffs without embedding personal information.
5. Produce plain-language drafts and improvement artifacts for formal review.

## Deliverables

- Pediatric pathway and handoff diagram
- Aggregate service-quality analysis
- Family communication draft with readability check

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not assess a child, recommend a procedure, or direct care.
Minors' information requires authorized handling and licensed human review.

## Starter prompt

Use the pediatric surgery quality toolpack. Work only with fictional or properly
de-identified aggregate information and apply heightened privacy standards for
minors. Verify age bands, denominators, follow-up windows, and uncertainty. Do
not assess a child, select a procedure, or direct care. Produce clear editable
artifacts and mark every clinical or family-facing statement for review by the
responsible pediatric surgical team.
