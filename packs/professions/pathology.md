# Pathology laboratory quality toolpack

Use this pack for de-identified laboratory turnaround, quality-control analysis,
specimen workflow mapping, evidence review, and report-template governance.

## Workflow

1. Define specimen class, process boundary, timestamp semantics, and owner.
2. Confirm de-identification and validate accession, duplicate, and unit rules.
3. Analyze aggregate turnaround, rework, quality controls, or process variation.
4. Map pre-analytic, analytic, and post-analytic controls and escalation paths.
5. Produce controlled analysis, diagrams, and document drafts for approval.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible laboratory quality analysis
- Editable specimen-flow and control diagram
- Versioned report-template or procedure draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not interpret a specimen, assign a diagnosis, or issue a
patient result. Authorized laboratory and pathologist review is mandatory.

## Starter prompt

Use the pathology laboratory quality toolpack. Work only with fictional or
properly de-identified aggregate information. Validate specimen grain,
timestamps, units, duplicates, denominators, and quality-control rules. Do not
interpret specimens, assign diagnoses, or issue patient results. Separate data,
inference, and professional judgment, and deliver reproducible editable
artifacts for authorized laboratory and pathologist review.
