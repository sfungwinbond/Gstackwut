# Petroleum engineering analysis toolpack

Use this pack for production-data analysis, decline and scenario models, economic
sensitivities, evidence review, process mapping, and decision communication.

## Workflow

1. Define asset, wells, interval, measurement basis, units, and data cutoff.
2. Preserve raw data and reconcile volumes, downtime, allocation, and ownership.
3. Model scenarios with uncertainty, sensitivities, and physical constraints.
4. Document environmental, integrity, regulatory, and commercial assumptions.
5. Deliver reproducible analysis for multidisciplinary technical review.

## Deliverables

- Reconciled production and data-quality report
- Editable scenario and sensitivity model
- Assumption register and decision-review package

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not set live operating parameters, certify reserves, authorize
field work, or replace subsurface, facilities, integrity, HSE, and legal review.
