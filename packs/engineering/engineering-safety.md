# Health and safety engineering toolpack

Use this pack for incident-data analysis, hazard registers, control mapping,
assurance evidence, standards research, and corrective-action tracking.

## Workflow

1. Define system, work scope, lifecycle phase, jurisdiction, and accountable owner.
2. Preserve incident evidence and distinguish exposure, event, and consequence.
3. Analyze aggregate patterns without hiding reporting or denominator limits.
4. Map hazards, controls, verification, degradation, and escalation ownership.
5. Track actions to objective closure evidence and independent review.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible incident or exposure analysis
- Editable hazard and control register
- Assurance plan and corrective-action evidence log

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not certify a workplace or product, replace a formal hazard
analysis, issue emergency instructions, or authorize hazardous work.

## Starter prompt

Use the health and safety engineering toolpack. State system, work scope,
lifecycle, jurisdiction, exposure basis, and governing criteria. Preserve
incident provenance, show denominator and reporting limits, and map hazards to
controls, verification, degradation, and owners. Do not certify safety, replace
a formal hazard analysis, issue emergency instructions, or authorize work.
Deliver controlled evidence for workers, engineers, HSE, legal, and owner review.
