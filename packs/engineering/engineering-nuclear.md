# Nuclear engineering evidence toolpack

Use this pack for non-operational evidence review, requirement traceability,
quality data analysis, controlled-document support, and safety-review preparation.

## Workflow

1. State facility or system scope, configuration, classification, and authority.
2. Use approved information, preserve provenance, and respect access controls.
3. Trace requirements, analyses, tests, assumptions, and unresolved conditions.
4. Quantify uncertainty and distinguish calculation, inference, and decision.
5. Produce controlled drafts for independent safety and regulatory review.

## Deliverables

- Controlled evidence and requirement matrix
- Reproducible non-operational analysis
- Safety-review draft with assumptions and unresolved items

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not provide operating instructions, calculate live setpoints,
handle controlled nuclear information, or authorize a safety-significant action.

## Starter prompt

Use the nuclear engineering evidence toolpack only for non-operational work with
approved information. State scope, configuration, classification, units,
authority, and source provenance. Trace requirements to calculations and tests,
quantify uncertainty, and expose unresolved conditions. Do not provide operating
instructions, live setpoints, controlled nuclear information, or authorization
for safety-significant work. Require independent engineering, safety, security,
quality, and regulatory review.
