# Biomedical engineering toolpack

Use this pack for design-control evidence, requirement traceability, verification
analysis, risk-file support, literature review, and controlled documentation.

## Workflow

1. Define device, intended use, revision, jurisdiction, lifecycle, and authority.
2. Protect health information and preserve approved evidence provenance.
3. Trace user needs through requirements, risks, controls, and verification.
4. Analyze verification data with protocol deviations and uncertainty visible.
5. Produce controlled drafts for independent quality and regulatory review.

## Deliverables

- Design-input and verification traceability matrix
- Reproducible verification analysis
- Editable risk-control and design-review package

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not diagnose, provide patient-specific advice, certify a
device, establish clinical safety, or authorize manufacturing or market release.

## Starter prompt

Use the biomedical engineering toolpack. State device, intended use, revision,
jurisdiction, lifecycle phase, standards baseline, and evidence provenance.
Protect health information and trace needs to requirements, risks, controls, and
verification. Show deviations and uncertainty. Do not diagnose, advise a patient,
certify a device, establish clinical safety, or authorize release. Deliver
controlled evidence for engineering, clinical, quality, and regulatory review.
