# Electrical and electronics engineering toolpack

Use this pack for interface control, power and tolerance budgets, test analysis,
verification matrices, fault review, and design communication.

## Workflow

1. Fix design revision, interfaces, units, tolerances, loads, and requirements.
2. Trace requirements to calculations, simulations, inspections, and tests.
3. Analyze test results with calibration and measurement uncertainty visible.
4. Map power, signals, grounding assumptions, dependencies, and fault paths.
5. Produce a review package with exceptions and release gates.

## Deliverables

- Interface and verification matrix
- Editable budget and tolerance workbook
- Reproducible test analysis and architecture diagrams

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not produce construction-ready schematics, certify electrical
safety or EMC, energize equipment, or authorize design release.

## Starter prompt

Use the electrical and electronics engineering toolpack. State revision,
interfaces, units, tolerances, loads, requirements, and evidence sources. Trace

calibration and uncertainty. Do not produce construction-ready schematics,
