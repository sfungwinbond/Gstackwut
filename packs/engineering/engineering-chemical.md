# Chemical engineering analysis toolpack

Use this pack for process-data reconciliation, mass and energy balance support,
scenario models, hazard-review preparation, scale-up evidence, and reporting.

## Workflow

1. Define process revision, streams, basis, units, boundaries, and data cutoff.
2. Preserve raw data and close balances with residuals and uncertainty visible.
3. Model bounded scenarios and distinguish correlations from validated physics.
4. Map safeguards, deviations, assumptions, and required hazard-review actions.
5. Deliver reproducible calculations and controlled review artifacts.

## Deliverables

- Reconciled process-data and balance workbook
- Bounded scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Editable process, safeguard, and review diagrams

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not set live process conditions, provide a production recipe,
complete a formal hazard study, or authorize scale-up or plant operation.

## Starter prompt

Use the chemical engineering analysis toolpack. State process revision,
boundaries, streams, basis, units, conditions, and source provenance. Preserve
raw data, show balance residuals and uncertainty, and distinguish empirical
correlations from validated physics. Do not set live conditions, provide a
production recipe, complete a formal hazard study, or authorize operation.
Deliver reproducible evidence for process, controls, HSE, quality, and plant review.
