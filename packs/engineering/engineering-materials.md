# Materials engineering toolpack

Use this pack for material-test analysis, qualification matrices, specification
comparison, failure evidence, lot variation, and review documentation.

## Workflow

1. Define material, lot, condition, process history, method, units, and criteria.
2. Preserve raw results and record specimen preparation and instrument calibration.
3. Analyze distributions, censoring, outliers, uncertainty, and batch variation.
4. Compare specifications and evidence without extrapolating beyond tested bounds.
5. Produce qualification evidence and unresolved-risk notes for approval.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible material-test analysis
- Qualification and specification matrix
- Failure-evidence or selection-review draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not certify a material, assign an allowables value, approve a
substitution, or replace laboratory, design, quality, and regulatory review.

Use the materials engineering toolpack. State material, lot, condition, process
