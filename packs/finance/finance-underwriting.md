# Insurance underwriting support toolpack

Use this pack for aggregate portfolio analysis, appetite and guideline mapping,
scenario testing, wording comparison, and review-ready underwriting evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define product, jurisdiction, effective date, portfolio, and decision criteria.
2. Minimize personal data and validate exposure, claim, and policy observation grain.
3. Analyze aggregate frequency, severity, concentration, and scenario sensitivity.
4. Compare approved wording and guidelines with dated authoritative sources.
5. Deliver evidence and exceptions for authorized underwriting review.

## Deliverables

- Aggregate portfolio and scenario workbook
- Guideline and wording comparison table
- Exception register and underwriting-review draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not bind coverage, set a premium, decide an individual risk,
or provide insurance, actuarial, legal, or regulatory advice.
