# Financial examination toolpack

Use this pack for regulatory-change research, examination planning, evidence
inventories, transparent sampling, control testing, and issue tracking.

## Workflow

1. Fix jurisdiction, legal entity, examination period, authority, and criteria.
2. Inventory evidence with provenance, custody, completeness, and access limits.
3. Define a reproducible sample and retain the population and selection logic.
4. Test controls against explicit criteria and separate fact from conclusion.
5. Track findings, owner responses, remediation evidence, and closure approval.

## Deliverables

- Evidence request and provenance register
- Reproducible sample and control-test workbook
- Issue log and review-ready examination draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not issue a regulatory conclusion, legal opinion, or official
finding. Applicable authorities and authorized examiners control the outcome.

## Starter prompt

Use the financial examination toolpack. State jurisdiction, authority, entity,
period, criteria, and data cutoff. Preserve evidence provenance and access
controls, define reproducible sampling, and distinguish observed fact, criteria,
inference, and proposed finding. This is not legal advice and does not issue an
official regulatory conclusion. Produce auditable working papers for authorized
examiner, legal, compliance, and accountable-owner review.
