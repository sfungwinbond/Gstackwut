# Credit analysis toolpack

Use this pack for normalized borrower analysis, covenant scenarios, portfolio
monitoring, source-led diligence, and credit-memo drafts.

## Workflow

1. Define borrower, facility, reporting date, currency, and policy framework.
2. Reconcile source statements and separate reported, adjusted, and forecast data.
3. Calculate transparent leverage, coverage, liquidity, and covenant scenarios.
4. Test downside cases and document collateral, concentration, and data limits.
5. Draft balanced findings with policy exceptions and approval gates.

## Deliverables

- Reconciled spreading and ratio workbook
- Downside and covenant sensitivity model
- Sourced credit-memo draft and exception log

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not approve or deny credit, set pricing, determine eligibility,
or replace fair-lending, legal, policy, and authorized credit review.
