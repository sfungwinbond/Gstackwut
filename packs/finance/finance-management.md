# Financial management toolpack

Use this pack for integrated planning, forecast scenarios, variance analysis,
management reporting, close controls, and board-ready decision materials.

## Workflow

1. Lock period, entity, currency, accounting basis, and source-system scope.
2. Reconcile inputs and separate actuals, assumptions, forecasts, and judgments.
3. Build formula-driven scenarios with sensitivities and control totals.
4. Explain variances, liquidity implications, risks, and decision gates.
5. Deliver editable models and controlled reports for accountable review.

## Deliverables

- Integrated forecast and scenario workbook
- Variance and control report
- Editable management or board presentation

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not certify accounts, issue investment advice, or replace
controller, auditor, tax, legal, or fiduciary review.

## Starter prompt

Use the financial management toolpack. Preserve source data, define period,
entity, currency, accounting basis, and observation grain, and reconcile every
control total before modeling. Separate actuals, assumptions, forecasts, and
judgment. Build editable formulas and traceable scenarios. This is not financial,
tax, legal, or investment advice; flag jurisdictional issues and require review
by the accountable finance owner before any decision or external reporting.
