# Investment analysis toolpack

Use this pack for source-led diligence, comparable-company analysis, transparent
valuation scenarios, thesis testing, and investment-committee draft materials.

## Workflow

1. Define security, entity, valuation date, currency, and decision question.
2. Prefer primary filings and reconcile reported, adjusted, and estimated data.
3. Build editable valuation cases with sensitivities and explicit assumptions.
4. Test the thesis against downside cases, conflicts, and disconfirming evidence.
5. Deliver a sourced model and balanced committee draft.

## Deliverables

- Source ledger and normalized financial table
- Formula-driven valuation and sensitivity model
- Thesis, risks, catalysts, and disconfirming-evidence memo

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not recommend buying or selling a security, execute trades,
or replace regulated research, compliance, or investment-committee review.

## Starter prompt

Use the investment analysis toolpack. Define the entity, instrument, valuation
date, currency, and decision. Prefer current primary filings, reconcile reported
and adjusted figures, and cite every material external claim. Build editable
valuation cases with transparent sensitivities and actively seek disconfirming
evidence. This is not investment advice and does not recommend or execute a
trade. Deliver a balanced draft for compliance and investment-committee review.
