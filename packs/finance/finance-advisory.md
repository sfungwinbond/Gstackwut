# Financial advisory support toolpack

Use this pack for educational scenario modeling, policy comparison, meeting
preparation, assumption documentation, and client-ready draft materials.

## Workflow

1. State jurisdiction, planning horizon, currency, objectives, and constraints.
2. Minimize personal data and distinguish user facts from assumed scenarios.
3. Model cash flows, ranges, fees, taxes, and sensitivity without hidden inputs.
4. Cite current official rules and label dates, conflicts, and uncertainty.
5. Produce plain-language drafts with alternatives and licensed-review gates.

## Deliverables

- Editable planning scenario workbook
- Source and assumption ledger
- Plain-language education or meeting draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not provide personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal
advice and does not recommend a specific security or product.

## Starter prompt

Use the financial advisory support toolpack. State the jurisdiction, currency,
planning horizon, objectives, and data cutoff. Minimize personal information and
separate facts from assumptions. Show fees, taxes, uncertainty, and alternative
scenarios transparently. This is not personalized financial, investment, tax, or
legal advice and must not recommend a specific security or product. Produce an
editable educational draft for review by an appropriately licensed professional.
