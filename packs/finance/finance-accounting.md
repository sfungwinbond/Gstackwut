# Accounting and audit support toolpack

Use this pack for reconciliations, close schedules, rollforwards, control testing,
evidence indexes, exception analysis, and financial-statement support.

## Workflow

1. Define entity, period, ledger, accounting framework, and materiality context.
2. Preserve source evidence and reconcile subledgers, schedules, and control totals.
3. Document entries, adjustments, classifications, and unresolved differences.
4. Test selected controls with reproducible populations and samples.
5. Produce controlled workpapers with preparer and reviewer signoffs.

## Deliverables

- Formula-driven reconciliation or rollforward
- Evidence index and exception register
- Review-ready close or audit workpaper package

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not issue an audit opinion, certify financial statements, post
entries, or replace professional accounting, tax, legal, or auditor review.

## Starter prompt

Use the accounting and audit support toolpack. Define entity, period, ledger,
framework, materiality context, source systems, and control totals. Preserve
evidence provenance, reconcile all schedules, and make adjustments and unresolved
differences explicit. This is not accounting, audit, tax, or legal advice and
does not issue an opinion or post entries. Deliver reproducible workpapers with
clear preparer, reviewer, and accountable-owner signoffs.
