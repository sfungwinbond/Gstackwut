# Budget analysis toolpack

Use this pack for baseline construction, driver-based budgets, forecast updates,
variance analysis, funding scenarios, and decision narratives.

## Workflow

1. Define entity, fund, period, currency, accounting basis, and approval calendar.
2. Reconcile actuals and commitments to authoritative source totals.
3. Build driver-based baseline, request, downside, and constraint scenarios.
4. Explain price, volume, timing, scope, and one-time variance components.
5. Deliver editable schedules and an assumptions-led recommendation draft.

## Deliverables

- Reconciled baseline and scenario workbook
- Variance bridge and funding-gap analysis
- Decision narrative with assumptions and approval gates

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not authorize spending, certify appropriations, or replace
accounting, procurement, grants, legal, or executive review.
