# Financial risk toolpack

Use this pack for exposure inventories, limit monitoring, scenario stress tests,
model validation evidence, control mapping, and risk-committee communication.

## Workflow

1. Define portfolio, valuation timestamp, units, hierarchy, and risk horizon.
2. Reconcile positions and market inputs before calculating any measure.
3. Run transparent base, stress, and reverse-stress scenarios.
4. Document model limits, nonlinearities, missing data, and concentration.
5. Produce a reproducible risk pack with thresholds and escalation owners.

## Deliverables

- Reconciled exposure and stress-test workbook
- Reproducible analysis with model-limit notes
- Risk register, control map, and committee deck

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not authorize trading, set limits, value an instrument for
official books, or provide investment advice. Independent review is required.
