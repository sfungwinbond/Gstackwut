# Lending operations toolpack

Use this pack for pipeline analysis, document checklists, process mapping,
educational payment scenarios, disclosure drafts, and service-quality metrics.

## Workflow

1. Define product, jurisdiction, policy version, period, and authorized owner.
2. Minimize personal data and separate educational inputs from verified records.
3. Model transparent payment scenarios with rates, fees, dates, and assumptions.
4. Map application, verification, decision, disclosure, and escalation controls.
5. Produce drafts and metrics for compliance and lending-authority review.

## Deliverables

- Pipeline and service-quality analysis
- Editable educational scenario worksheet
- Process map, checklist, and disclosure draft

## Safety boundary

This toolpack does not determine eligibility, approve or deny a loan, set a rate,
or provide credit, financial, legal, or fair-lending advice.
